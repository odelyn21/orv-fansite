"This story… only I have read it." Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint, Prologue

Broadcast Loading

Ten years ago, an average office worker named Kim Dokja started reading a web novel called Three Ways to Survive in a Ruined World. He read it until the very last chapter, alone, all 3,149 of them.

Then reality became the novel. The scenarios began. And suddenly, being the only reader who knew what happens next was the most dangerous power in the world.

This is a fan site for the constellations, sponsors, incarnations, and the story only he knows how to survive.
